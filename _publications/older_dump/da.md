venue: "Under submission"
year: 2026
tldr: "We propose Flow Proposal Particle Filters (FPPF), which use a conditional generative model to learn near-optimal proposals for particle filters. By conditioning particle propagation on observations, FPPF steers particles toward high-posterior regions before weighting, reducing weight variance and mitigating particle degeneracy in high-dimensional nonlinear systems, while retaining exact Bayesian filtering via tractable likelihoods."
