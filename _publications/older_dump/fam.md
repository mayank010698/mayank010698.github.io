tldr: "In this work, we study stochastic optimal control formulations of generative modeling, where sampling is framed as learning a controlled stochastic process. We show that Adjoint Matching performs descent on a surrogate objective and can become unstable due to uncontrolled distribution shift between path distributions. To address this, we propose Fisher Adjoint Matching (FAM), a natural-gradient method that approximates a KL trust-region update, yielding more stable and efficient training of generative models."
